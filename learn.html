◐ Personality Type

Based on Carl Jung’s idea that people have natural preferences in four areas. Each area is a pair, and you get one letter from each pair, making 16 possible types (for example INFP or ESTJ):

  • E or I — Extraversion or Introversion: do you get energy from people or from quiet?
  • S or N — Sensing or Intuition: do you focus on facts and details, or patterns and possibilities?
  • T or F — Thinking or Feeling: do you decide mainly with logic or with values and care for people?
  • J or P — Judging or Perceiving: do you prefer plans and closure, or flexibility and options?

Everyone uses both sides of each pair; your type shows which side feels more natural. Types describe preferences, not abilities or worth.

✝ Spiritual Gifts

The Bible teaches that the Holy Spirit gives every believer gifts to serve others and build up the church (Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 12; Ephesians 4:11–12; 1 Peter 4:10–11). Gifts are different from natural talents or personality, although they often work together.

A test can point you in a direction, but gifts are best discovered by serving, praying and listening to what mature believers see in you.

✦ Birth Chart

A birth chart is a map of where the Sun, Moon and planets were at the moment and place you were born. Astrology is an old tradition that reads this map as symbols. Selah explains it in simple words:

  • Planets are the “what”: parts of life, like feelings (Moon) or love (Venus).
  • Signs are the “how”: the style, like boldly (Aries) or gently (Pisces).
  • Houses are the “where”: twelve areas of life, worked out from your birth time and place.
  • Aspects are the “conversation”: angles between planets.

Astrology is a symbolic tradition, not a science and not a source of guidance. Selah uses it only as a mirror for reflection, and every section points back to Christ.

Holding results lightly

Test everything; hold on to what is good (1 Thessalonians 5:21). Use what rings true as a prompt for prayer and growth, and let go of what doesn’t. God’s Word, not a result, is the final word on who you are.
