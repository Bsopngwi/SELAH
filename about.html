About Selah

Selah is a word found often in the Psalms. Many understand it as an invitation to pause and reflect.

Selah is a self-reflection space. It brings together tools people use to understand themselves (a personality type test, a spiritual gifts test and a birth chart) and explains each one in simple words.

Every result ends the same way: by pointing back to Jesus Christ. These tools can help you notice how you are made, but only God can tell you who you are.

Your privacy

Your answers and results stay in your own browser on your device. Selah has no accounts and does not send your results anywhere. When you create a birth chart, the place name you type is sent to a free place-search service (Open-Meteo) to find its location and time zone.
